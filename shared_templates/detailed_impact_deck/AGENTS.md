# AGENTS.md — shared_templates/detailed_impact_deck

## 🎯 Purpose & Scope
This directory hosts the specialized template and design system for the **10-Presentation Ecosystem Impact Series** (`detailed_overall_impact_presentations/`).

---

## 🎨 Visual Identity & Differentiating Markers
This series is engineered to be **instantly recognizable and distinct** from the Overview Presentations, future Technical deep-dives, and Explainer decks:

1. **🌿 Corner Ribbon (Top-Right):**
   - Fixed angled badge `🌿 ЭКОСИСТЕМА` in the participant's signature sector color.
2. **⚡ 6px Left Vertical Neon Stripe:**
   - Runs the full height of the 1920x1080 canvas with active ambient glow.
3. **👤 Hero Stakeholder Badge (Top-Left):**
   - Explicit track badge on every slide: `[ 👤 УЧАСТНИК 01: ГРАЖДАНЕ И СЕМЬИ ]`.
4. **🎨 10-Color Signature Sector Palette:**
   - `theme-citizens`: Electric Cyan (`#00f0ff`)
   - `theme-security`: Crimson Shield (`#ff2a5f`)
   - `theme-business`: Emerald Green (`#00e676`)
   - `theme-advertisers`: Neon Magenta (`#e040fb`)
   - `theme-adtech`: Vibrant Amber (`#ffab00`)
   - `theme-government`: Federal Sapphire (`#2979ff`)
   - `theme-developers`: Cyber Lime (`#76ff03`)
   - `theme-fintech`: Digital Ruble Teal (`#1de9b6`)
   - `theme-synergies`: Prism Gold (`#ffd600`)
   - `theme-roadmap`: Titanium Silver-Blue (`#90caf9`)
5. **🎴 Specialized Card Archetypes:**
   - **Comparison Grids:** `.compare-legacy` (Red `✕`) vs `.compare-turbase` (Green `✓`).
   - **PROs / CONs / Mitigation Triptych:** `.pro-box` (Green), `.con-box` (Red), `.mitigation-panel` (Cyan banner).
   - **User Journey Storyboards:** 3-step concrete Day 1 onboarding flowcards.
   - **KPI Metric Grids:** Monospace numbers with glowing sector tint.
